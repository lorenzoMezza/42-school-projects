## What it is

A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline: documents are split, indexed and searched, and the most relevant passages are given to a language model to answer questions about them.

## What it shows

- Embeddings, indexing and retrieval
- Prompting a model with retrieved context
- Measuring answer quality
